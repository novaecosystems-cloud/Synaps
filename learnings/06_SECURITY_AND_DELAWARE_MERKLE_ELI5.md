### 🏛️ Why Do Big Corporations Care About "Delaware DGCL § 141"?
In the United States, almost all giant companies (Apple, Google, Tesla, Nike) are legally registered in the State of Delaware. 

Under **Delaware General Corporation Law § 141(e)**:
* Company directors are protected from being sued by angry shareholders IF they can prove they made careful decisions by relying on trusted expert reports and audited records.
