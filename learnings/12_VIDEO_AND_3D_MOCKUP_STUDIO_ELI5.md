## 📐 How Does the Screen Fit "Like a Glove"?

If you just paste a square photo onto a tilted laptop, it will look crooked and fake, with the corners poking out like an oversized cardboard box. 

To make it look 100% real, we use **Planar Math Magic (Corner-Pin Warping)**:
1. **Corner Tracking:** The computer tracks the 4 tiny corners of the real laptop glass across hundreds of video frames.
2. **Perspective Bending:** It bends and squeezes our Causarix app screen so it matches the tilt of the laptop at every fraction of a second.
3. **Smooth Glove Fit:** The app screen slides right inside the bezel, keeping the camera notch cutout and shiny glass reflections intact!
